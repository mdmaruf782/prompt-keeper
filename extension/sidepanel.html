Image to Video

On the Image to Video page, pick your reference images once, then press “Capture from page”. When you press Generate on the Image to Video page, the saved ids are written into the request’s inputs.refimg, in this order. The page needs at least one image selected to allow Generate.

Page status

Link results from History

On axcel.cloud's History list page, press the button. Each card's prompt is matched to your prompts, and its job id is linked to that prompt (the same as “View video”). Scroll the list first so the cards you want are loaded.

Results not in your prompts

History cards whose prompt is not in your lists get a “＋ Download list” button too. They are saved into the category chosen here, numbered by the EPISODE number in their prompt (or 1, 2, 3…).

Download list

On the History page each card gets a label (Project › Category › serial) and a “Download list” button. Added cards appear here, ordered by project, category and serial.

Download result

Open a finished result on axcel.cloud, then press the button. It clicks the site's own “Download for final result” button.

{serial} = the prompt's number in the category (always added if missing), {ep} = episode number from the prompt's title, {title} = the title after “EPISODE NN —”, {created} = Created date/time. Folders go under Chrome's Downloads folder: Project/Category/001 - Episode 28.mp4. The result's prompt must match a prompt in the selected category, otherwise nothing is downloaded.
